Nice to meet you

I’ve always been fascinated by visual arts, looking up to my artist older brother who inspired me to appreciate all things pleasing to the eye. UI/UX design combines my fascination with the arts with computer science and logic, blending into a field that trains my knowledge in psychology and computer science with my love for visual aesthetics. I’m particularly interested in creating accessible technologies for people of all abilities (case study about sonification systems coming soon).

When I’m not studying or designing, you can find me advocating Southeast Asian student retention and youth mental health, making matcha and other fun drinks for my friends, or playing video games (Deadlock is my current favorite)!
